## About me

I'm a first-year **Computer Science co-op student at McMaster University**. I like building across the stack, from figuring out how a system should work to making its interface feel right.

For me, the best part is turning an idea into something someone else can open, try, and use.

- 🚀 **Building:** [PlacdAI](https://placdai.com), an AI-powered way to visualize changes to your space.
- 🧪 **Previously:** QA intern at **vFairs**, testing AI-powered features and documenting bugs.
- 🧠 **Interested in:** Product engineering, developer tools, applied AI, and thoughtful design.
- 💼 **Looking ahead:** Summer 2027 software engineering internships and opportunities to collaborate.

## Projects

### 🏠 [PlacdAI](https://placdai.com) — See what your space could become

An AI interior design product that lets people experiment with changes to their own rooms and discover furniture to shop for. I built and launched the MVP, connecting room-image editing with product discovery.

`React` `TanStack Start` `Supabase` `Google Gemini`

**[Explore the live product →](https://placdai.com)**

`React` `Node.js` `Express` `MongoDB`
